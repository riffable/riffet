Changes from the SVG assets: the elements of each glyph are folded into one path (circles, rects
and polylines become arcs and lines) and embedded in Compose ImageVectors by
scripts/gen-mobile-icons.ts. The Menu and Brand drawings are Riffet's own and do not come from
Lucide.
